Free Data Tools for Sacramento-Area Nonprofits

Built by Quiana Anderson, a UC Berkeley-trained researcher, for organizations that can't afford a full-time evaluator or data team.

Small nonprofits are constantly asked for outcome data by funders — but rarely have the budget for a program evaluator. These free tools are built to help close that gap: honest, funder-ready reporting that's upfront about what your data can and can't prove.

Sacramento Service Gap Map

An interactive map showing where poverty, transportation access, and nonprofit services overlap — and where they don't — across the greater Sacramento region.

Impact Report Generator

Upload your program's intake or survey data and get a polished, funder-ready PDF report — complete with outcome charts and clear methodological caveats, generated automatically.
